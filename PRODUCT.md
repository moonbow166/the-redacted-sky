# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People curious about UAP, including first-time visitors with no subject knowledge and people who do not speak English as their first language. Experienced visitors can search and inspect original records.

## Product Purpose

Make public UAP records approachable through real media and question-led exploration. Success means a visitor notices something, understands its context and limitations, and finds a question worth following.

## Positioning

An independent evidence experience that connects original materials to approachable editorial paths, while keeping source statements, interpretation, and uncertainty distinct.

## Operating Context

Visitors can enter the immersive Experience or Explore Archive. Archive supports guided exploration and a searchable catalogue. The current project is interest-led and free, with no monetization requirement.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing Experience and its navigation during the official-data and first-path update.
- Rebuild data from official sources with field-level provenance and honest missing-data handling. Remove third-party dependencies only when genuinely replaced; historical distributed versions retain their credits.
- Build the first complete path around the 1952 Tremonton film. Astronaut accounts and camera interpretation are subsequent paths, not required simultaneous launches.
- Preserve stable case URLs and search access.
- My sky is a gentle, device-local exploration prototype: keep selected official materials, compare them, and make explicitly personal connections. No timer, score, correct-answer gate, or dragging is required. Shared boards and cross-device accounts remain deferred.
- Donations, domain purchase, social posting, and further 3D asset production are deferred.

## Brand Commitments

The Redacted Sky. Preserve the established immersive identity. Use approachable English, sentence case, short copy, and no em dashes in authored interface text. Reconstructions are labeled interpretations, never evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing records and provenance live under data/. The official PURSUE archive is the source for the independent rebuild. Existing snapshots include third-party descriptions and translations and must not be relabeled as independently sourced.

## Product Principles

1. Let visitors encounter evidence before explanations.
2. Organize guided paths around questions, not file formats.
3. Make claims traceable to a source and locator.
4. Keep unresolved questions open without manufacturing mystery or certainty.
5. Let visual guidance and progressive disclosure lower the reading burden.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

English remains the interface language for this iteration. Short sentences, visible media, readable type, keyboard-operable controls, responsive layouts, and optional deeper reading should support non-native readers. Do not require audio, motion, or hover to understand the content.
